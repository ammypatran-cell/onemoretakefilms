TIPS
----
- Save as .jpg. If you have .png or .webp, rename the extension in
  index.html too, or just re-export as .jpg — easiest.
- Keep each file under ~400 KB so the site loads fast on mobile data.
  (squoosh.app is free and does this in the browser.)
- The films grid images get a slight black & white treatment that turns
  to full colour on hover — that's on purpose, it's a film look.
- To swap what a photo means, edit the <h3>, <p> and <span class="tag">
  text for that card in index.html.
