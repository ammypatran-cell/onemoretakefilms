01 — Who we are

Two people, one camera,
and a lot of patience.

We're a small filmmaking duo out of Punjab. One of us frames the shot, the other one paints — and somewhere between those two habits is how we see a day: light first, story second, everything else after.

No fifteen-person crew. No shouting. We show up early, stay till the last dance, and shoot until it feels right — which is usually one more take than everybody expected. That's the whole name.
