What to do with the files

  1. Download each one (or hit Download all).
  2. Move them from your Downloads folder into demo\assets\img\.
  3. Open index.html and refresh — the placeholders are replaced by the real photos.

Tip: use his original camera files rather than photos saved off Instagram — Instagram re-compresses everything, and it shows badly on the full-screen hero image.
